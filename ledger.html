夜榜 The Ledger

Every night here was replayed on the server before it was written down. A run gets on the ledger by being reproducible from the decisions the player actually made — not by claiming a score.

這裡的每一夜，都先在伺服器上重跑過一遍，才記下來。能上榜的，是那些能從玩家真正做過的選擇裡重現出來的一夜——不是自己報一個分數就算。
