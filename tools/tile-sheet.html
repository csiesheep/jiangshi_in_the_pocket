Art pass 1 · twenty tiles

Jiangshi in the Pocket

The village has locked its doors, and at the far end of it is the 義莊. Every scene here is built on one of two shells, so the halves stay apart without a caption: indoors gets beams, plaster and a stone floor under a single oil lamp; outdoors gets sky, the same moon in the same corner, and mist on cold ground. Silhouette, staging and light are settled in this pass; texture, wear and the small props come next.

The door plan

The small square on each card is that tile’s own walls. Gold is a way through; teal is the seam the hillside joins the village along. Tiles rotate freely as they are placed, so only the count and the arrangement matter.

The world cast

The board tints every scene by which half you are standing in — amber indoors, moon-teal outdoors. Switch it off to see the art underneath it.

What is not here

The event pool, the item pool and the King are still open in the design record, so no card says what a search finds. Only the map is settled.
