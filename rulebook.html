The night

You are the caretaker. The village has locked its doors, and at the far end of it is the 義莊 — the corpse hostel, where coffins wait for the road home. One of them has waited too long.

It is 9:00 PM. At midnight the third watch sounds and the thing in that coffin comes for you. You have until then to do one of two things:

  1. Lay him to rest. Find his 神主牌 — the ancestral tablet — in the 停柩房 Sealed Crypt, carry it out of the village, and bury it in the 亂葬崗 Mass Grave.
  2. Meet him. Be standing there properly armed when he arrives — and it is only what you are holding that decides it.

Either ends the night. So does dying.

What you track

Twenty tiles, ten indoor and ten outdoor. An event pool and an item pool, kept separate. Thirteen items, in five kinds.

Four numbers: 血 Health, 攻擊力 Attack, the 回合 turn you are on, and your 躲藏額度 cower charges.

Setup

  1. The 門廳 Gatehouse goes down first. The 後門石階 Back Steps is set aside — it goes down later, the moment you first step outside.
  2. The rest are shuffled into two face-down stacks, indoor and outdoor.
  3. You start with Attack 1, Health 6, 3 cower charges and three 糯米.
  4. The clock reads 9:00 PM. It is turn 1 of 30.

You begin in the Gatehouse and draw no event for it. The night starts when you move.

The clock

The turn is the clock. There is no deck to run down.

The game is exactly 30 turns, each of them six minutes. Turn 1 begins at nine; turn 30 ends at midnight. The three hour bands are ten turns each.

BandTurnsClock
9 PM1–1021:00 → 22:00
10 PM11–2022:00 → 23:00
11 PM21–3023:00 → 24:00

Events get worse as the bands advance. There is no losing to the clock — midnight is not a deadline you fail, it is an appointment you keep.

Taking a turn

Every turn is one action, then an event, then an optional search.

1 — Choose one action

移動 MOVE — step into an adjacent room, explored or not. If it is unexplored, a tile goes down.

停留 STAY — remain where you are. Legal, and often correct: staying is how you search a room a second time.

躲藏 COWER — hide. Spend a charge and end the turn immediately: no event, no search. See Cowering.

Movement is optional — but standing still costs a turn and draws an event exactly like walking does. There is no free turn.

2 — Place the tile, if the room is new

The top tile of the matching stack goes down. Rotated however it likes, with one constraint: one of its ways out must line up with the way you just came through. Its other edges match nothing in particular — a door may open onto a blank wall, and a wall may seal off a neighbour's door.

There is no fixed footprint. The village sprawls as far as the tiles take it.

3 — Draw an event

One event, read for the current hour band. Always — including when you re-enter a room you know, and including when you stayed put.

4 — Search, if the room offers it

Ten of the twenty places can be searched, each for one category of thing.

CategoryWhere
武器 weapons鐵匠鋪 Blacksmith · 柴房 Woodshed · 牌坊 Memorial Arch
符咒 talismans經堂 Sutra Hall · 靈堂 Mourning Hall · 竹林 Bamboo Grove
丹藥 medicine藥鋪 Apothecary · 帳房 Counting Room · 槐樹 Pagoda Tree
法器 implement土地廟 Earth God Shrine — and nowhere else

Searching is free — it costs no turn. It happens after the event, and it may find nothing. If it does, you can search the same room again by spending next turn on STAY — which means another event. That is the price of rummaging: not the search, the lingering.

You may carry 6 items. The 神主牌 does not count against that.

5 — End of turn

帳房 Counting Room and 槐樹 Pagoda Tree give +1 Health if you finish your turn standing in them. Then the clock advances six minutes.

What the night throws at you

Every draw is one of six things, and the mix sours as the night goes on: more fights, bigger ones, and at eleven o'clock the small mercies stop.

EventWhat happens
殭屍A pack of them, three to six. See Fighting
損血−1 Health. Bad footing, a dropped beam, the cold
回血+1 Health. Nothing above ten o'clock is generous, and by eleven this stops entirely
沒事Nothing happens. Rarer every hour
中毒Corpse-poison, below

中毒 — poisoned

屍毒 gets into you, and it does not wear off.

−1 Health at the start of every turn, until a 糯米 draws it out.

Nothing else cures it, and it does not stack — poisoned is poisoned, however often it happens. The 護身符 does not help: poison is not a wound, and the charm only softens wounds.

Left alone it will kill you. The rice in your pack is the only answer — which is exactly why you can never comfortably spend it on anything else.

村民受傷 — a villager is hurt

Someone from the village is bitten and bleeding. Spend a 糯米 to draw the poison out of them, and they give you what they have. Refuse — or have no rice — and you watch them turn, and then you fight them.

BandSave them, and takeRefuse, and fight
9 PM護身符 — every wound 1 less, all night殭屍 4
10 PM真火符殭屍 5
11 PM五雷符殭屍 6

The rice you keep for yourself is rice you don't have when someone needs it — and late in the night the villagers are carrying exactly what you were searching for. Rice is also the only thing that cures 中毒. Poisoned, with one 糯米 left and a bleeding villager in front of you, is the hardest choice the game asks.

Fighting, and not fighting

No dice. The arithmetic is the whole of it:

Health lost = (number of 殭屍) − (your Attack)

Never more than 4 from one fight, and never a gain. That ceiling is why a very high Attack stops helping against an ordinary pack — you can only get the damage down to zero.

逃跑 Running. On any 殭屍 you may run instead of fighting: −1 Health, and you step into an adjacent room you have already been in. You draw no event there and you do not search — the turn is over. Because you are no longer standing in the room you fled, a dead end there will not break its wall on you. Spending 黑狗血 does the same thing for free, which is what the blood is for.

護身符 softens 殭屍 wounds by 1 and nothing else — not the 損血 events, not 中毒, not the Health that running costs.

破牆 — when the wall gives

If a room leaves you with no unexplored way out, they come through the wall once the room's own event has been dealt with — three at nine o'clock, four at ten, five at eleven. You choose which wall.

So a dead end can cost you two fights in one turn, with no cowering between them. Late at night that is the worst thing that can happen to you in a single turn. The hole stays open afterwards, and you can use it.

Cowering

Cowering costs a whole turn and one of your 3 charges, and it is the only turn in the game that draws no event.

It does not heal you. What it buys is the draw you did not make — and late in the night that is worth more than a bandage. At eleven o'clock roughly seven draws in ten are a fight.

  • You may cower anywhere, indoors or out.
  • The 香堂 Incense Hall gives one charge back, once a night.
  • When the charges are gone, they are gone.

Three charges is the whole supply of not this time you will get. Spend them early and the last hour has nothing in it but you and the draw.

Going outside

The only way out of the village is the 天井 Courtyard's moon gate — one of its four ordinary ways out, not a fifth. Take it and the 後門石階 Back Steps goes down against the Courtyard, joined along that edge. Then the turn continues as normal.

The gate works both ways. Going back in costs a turn, like any move.

Almost everything you will ever carry is found indoors. Crossing the moon gate is a decision made with what you already have.

Items

You can carry 6. Only talismans stack — however many of one you pile up they share a single slot; everything else takes a slot each, so the three 糯米 you start with are already half your pack.

Weapons stay with you. Everything else — talismans, medicines, the banner — is used once and is gone.

武器 Weapons — kept

A weapon is your Attack, not a bonus on top of it. Only the best one you are holding counts: carrying two does not add them together.

WeaponAttack
戒刀 Precept Knife1
桃木劍 Peachwood Sword1
銅錢劍 Coin Sword2
七星劍 Seven-Star Sword3

There is only one of each weapon in the village. Turn up one you are already carrying and you find nothing — so the more blades you own, the more often a weapon search comes up empty. 七星劍 is rare on purpose: roughly one search in seven. Many nights you will fight with something worse, and that is the game working, not the game cheating you.

符咒 Talismans — burned once

A talisman adds to your sword. Bring both to the same fight and the two stack.

TalismanWhat it does
真火符 True FireFight at 1 — or raise one sword's Attack by +1, and it stays raised
五雷符 Five ThunderFight at 4
血符 BloodFight at 5. Writing it costs you 1 Health
硃砂 CinnabarCopy a talisman you already hold — +2 more of it, all sharing one slot

真火符 is the only one worth spending outside a fight: put it into a sword and the sword is better for the rest of the night. A sword takes only one — so there is a ceiling on how good steel alone ever gets. Talismans have no such limit: the same one can be found again and again, and a talisman search comes up empty only one time in ten.

丹藥 Medicine — taken once

Health stops at 10. Anything that would take you past it is wasted — so a 糯米 at 9 Health is worth holding rather than eating.

護符 The charm — kept

護身符 Protective Charm — 殭屍 wounds are 1 less, all night. Nothing else. It is not found by searching: the only way to get one is to save the villager in the 9 PM band, which makes it the earliest opportunity in the game and the one that pays off latest.

法器 The banner — used once

攝魂幡 Soul-Snatching Banner — doubles your sword for one fight. It does nothing for a talisman.

There is exactly one place it is ever found: 土地廟 the Earth God Shrine, and only sometimes, when you search there. Doubling a sword is wasted on a small pack — damage only goes down to zero, and a sword and talisman together often get you there anyway. Keep it for something big.

The two rites

The 停柩房 and the 亂葬崗 are the only rooms that ask for something beyond walking in.

停柩房 Sealed Crypt. Resolve the room's event as usual — then one more, which is the opening of the coffin itself. Survive it and still be standing there, and the 神主牌 is yours. Carrying it does not use an item slot.

亂葬崗 Mass Grave. The same shape, for the burial: the room's event, then one more for the digging. Survive that holding the 神主牌 and you have won.

Both are two events in one turn, and the second comes at the worst possible moment — standing over the hole with the tablet in your hands. Neither win is free.

Midnight — 三更

When turn 30 ends the third watch sounds, and 殭屍王 the King comes to whatever place you are standing in.

There is one exchange. He strikes once, you meet it once, and the night is decided. No fleeing, no cowering, no medicine, no second chance — and 黑狗血 does not work on him, because there is nowhere he is not.

Only what you are holding decides it. Nothing you did earlier counts here except what it left in your hands.

Unless you are not there to be found. The 溪澗 Stream is running water and he will not cross it. Stand there when turn 30 ends and he does not come at all — you live. If the 神主牌 is already in the ground you have won anyway; if it is not, you simply saw the night out.

You do not kill him. Nobody destroys a 殭屍王 — the most anyone has ever managed is to put one back.

Winning & losing

EndingHow
下葬 — the burialTake the 神主牌 from the 停柩房, carry it out through the moon gate, and finish the rite at the 亂葬崗. It ends the night the moment you finish it
見到天亮 — you saw the night outBe standing on the 溪澗 when turn 30 ends. You live; he is still out there
LostHealth reaches 0 — in a fight, from an event, or from 中毒 at the start of a turn

The burial is the win you plan for. It asks for turns spent walking: three particular rooms have to turn up, and the 天井 is the only way between the village and the hillside.

Midnight is the other way the night can end, and it asks for the opposite — turns spent standing still, and whatever you were able to find while you stood there. The game will not tell you whether what you are carrying is enough. That is the point of it.

There is no losing to the clock.
